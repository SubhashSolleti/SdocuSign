# Every letter, in its place.
Diwan is the diwan of a modern office: every letter in and out is registered with its Hijri date, routed to the position that answers it, and tracked on business time. Replies are generated, approved, signed, sealed and dispatched with proof, Arabic first, so no letter is lost.

## How correspondence moves

- 
### Register
Incoming, outgoing and internal registers by year, numbered at registration with your scheme, including Hijri years. Scanning is the first intake channel.
- 
### Route
Each hop is a task to a position, not a person: the work follows the holder, and escalation goes to the position above. Due dates run on business time with the Hijri calendar and the GCC weekend.
- 
### Reply
The letter is generated from a bilingual letterhead, approved through the approval matrix, signed by the signatory and sealed with the organisation's e-seal, archived as PDF/A.
- 
### Dispatch
Dispatch with cover sheets and a proof of dispatch; the thread links replies, references and forwards.

## Built for government practice

### Configuration packs
- Hijri calendar and numbering, Arabic numerals
- Reference schemes and classification labels
- Bilingual letterheads per department

### Positions
- Positions by department with their holders and validity
- Acting assignments as dated holders
- Escalation to the position above

### Records
- Classification from public to secret controls access
- Correspondents registered once, de-duplicated
- Retention per kind from the pack's schedules

## What is different about Diwan

- 
### Composed from the platform
Diwan adds the register, the desk and the letter; documents, workflow, generation, signing and AI are the platform's.
- 
### GCC specifics are configuration
Hijri numbering, reference schemes and letterheads come as a configuration pack, not a separate code path.
- 
### Two signatures on an outgoing letter
The signatory's signature and the organisation's e-seal, both PAdES, in the letter's sealed PDF.

## Questions

### When is Diwan available?+
The register, routing and the letter path are built; the remaining screens, reports and the demo are in progress. It is not part of the current trial.

### Can we use our own numbering?+
Yes. The pattern is a setting, with year, Hijri year, kind, department and sequence tokens.
