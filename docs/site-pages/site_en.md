## What has been reviewed, and what has not

### Reviewed
- The signing path read against OWASP ASVS 5.0.0: 22 items, 16 passed, 4 found and fixed, the rest recorded as follow-ups.
- Seals verified with an independent PDF tool; no unsigned signature field is left in a sealed file.
- Scale measured: 10,000 envelopes bulk sent in 5 min 21 s, 998 signers at once with no errors.

### Not yet
- Signatures are advanced, not qualified.
- No independent penetration test and no ISO 27001 certification yet; both are in the pre-release plan.
- No regional SaaS in the trial.Read security and compliance
