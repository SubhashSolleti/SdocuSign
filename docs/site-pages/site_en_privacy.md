## This website

This website is a set of static pages. It sets no cookies, stores nothing in your browser, runs no analytics and loads nothing from another service.

## The trial request

When you send the trial request form, we receive the organisation, your name, your work e-mail address, the product, your deployment preference and your message. We use them only to answer your request. They are stored on our own server and e-mailed to our team; they are not shared with anyone else.

To protect the form from abuse, our server counts requests per network address for a short time. The address is not stored with your request.

## The assistant

When you ask the assistant a question, the question, the page you are on and the earlier turns of the conversation go to our server and to the AI model we have configured, only to answer you. We do not store them: our server records that a call was made, with a fingerprint of the text rather than the text. If the model is a cloud provider's, the question passes through that provider under our agreement with it. As with the form, questions are counted per network address for a short time.

## Your rights

Write to info@soit.ae to ask what we hold about you, to correct it or to delete it.
