# Smart Orbit
Smart Orbit Information Technology LLC is a UAE-based technology company and business partner, registered in the United Arab Emirates. We work alongside organisations to turn information into intelligent business processes through one shared, extensible platform.

## What we build
One platform for documents, correspondence, e-signature and business apps, for government and enterprise organisations in the GCC and beyond.

## How we build
Arabic and English from the first screen. Deployment by choice: your servers, a regional service, or both. No third-party service in the build or at runtime. Measured numbers and an honest list of what is not done yet in every release.

## Our vision
An orbit of intelligence around your information, in your control, at every stage of AI.

## Our values
Ownership: your information is yours. Evidence: we measure, we cite, we admit. Partnership: your business first, built together. Clarity: one platform, no islands. Language: Arabic first, every tongue welcome.

## How we work
You bring the business. We bring the platform. We build the next step together: discover, co-design, deliver, review, with the architects in the room and findings fixed in days.
