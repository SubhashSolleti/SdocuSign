# One platform under everything you run
Documents, correspondence, signatures and apps share one content model, one workflow engine, one forms designer, one search, one audit trail and one licence. Others plug AI into products built before it existed; here the platform was built on AI, and the products on the platform. Where it runs, which AI it calls and what happens after an upload are your configuration, not a product edition.

## Trust
What has been reviewed, what has not, and where to read the detail.

### Reviewed
- The signing path read against OWASP ASVS 5.0.0: 22 items, 16 passed, 4 found and fixed, the rest recorded as follow-ups.
- Seals verified with an independent PDF tool; no unsigned signature field is left in a sealed file.
- Scale measured: 10,000 envelopes bulk sent in 5 min 21 s, 998 signers at once with no errors.

### Not yet
- Signatures are advanced, not qualified.
- No independent penetration test and no ISO 27001 certification yet; both are in the pre-release plan.
- No regional SaaS in the trial.
