## Three builders, one platform

- 
### Apps
Pages on a responsive 12-column grid with widgets, including widgets from TroveDocs, Diwan and Signora; one binding grammar; queries to your data sources.
- 
### Workflows
Events, gateways, tasks, timers and boundary events on one canvas, with a properties panel, a console and a run overlay.
- 
### Forms
Forms generated from a type or a task, edited in the designer, with sandboxed JavaScript, data-source controls and attachments.

## Connect and automate

### Integration
- Data sources in the workspace: databases, REST services and content types
- A connector framework with credentials kept in the secrets store
- Inbound webhooks and outbound calls

### Operations
- A published workflow can be called as a REST operation and as a tool for AI agents
- Every processing step is also a workflow node
- Runs, tasks and their history in the operations screens

### AI
- AI steps in workflows through the gateway
- An agent that designs apps and a copilot that edits them are planned

## What is different about Tameer
Low-code platforms build apps next to your documents. Tameer builds apps on them: the document travels form, governed folder, AI review, decision, signature and next team without a seam.

- 
### Your data, your infrastructure
Apps read your own databases and storage through workspace data sources; nothing is copied to a vendor service.
- 
### Products as building blocks
The products expose widgets, so a custom app can show a document, a register or an envelope without rebuilding it.
- 
### One engine everywhere
The workflows you build run on the same engine as the products' own routing and approvals.
